# Client-derived tables: notice

Generated by `tools/maps/client-tables.ts`. Do not edit these files by hand; regenerate them with
`pnpm tsx tools/maps/client-tables.ts` (it needs the pinned client at `WOW_INSTALL`) and check them
with `pnpm tsx tools/maps/client-tables.ts --check`; `manifest.json` records every file's SHA-256.

## What these files are

- `taxi.json`: the flight network of Eastern Kingdoms and Kalimdor (65 flight nodes, 286 flights
  between 143 node pairs, each with its 3D path length and its shape simplified to 25 yd) and the
  stops of 14 transport paths.
- `zones.json`: the faction group and the sanctuary flag of 54 zones.
- `dungeons.json`: 30 dungeon-finder rows with their tuning levels, and 51 dungeon and raid maps
  with their type.

They hold selected columns of tables of the World of Warcraft: Forever client, and a few values this
project computes from them (path lengths and simplified shapes). `manifest.json` and each file name
the table and column of every value. The meanings of some values (the faction and sanctuary bits,
the tuning level) are inferred and labelled as such.

## How they were made

- Read-only from the local client's `Data/` folder through this project's CASC reader
  (`tools/casc`), pinned to `wow_classic_beta` 1.60.1.70124, build key
  `dd3dfc2881c407299f46c2aaf34c130b`. Nothing is fetched from the network.
- Tables: `TaxiPathNode` (1000437), `TaxiPath` (1067802), `TaxiNodes` (1068100), `Map` (1349477),
  `AreaTable` (1353545), `LFGDungeons` (1361033), `UiMapAssignment` (1957219), `ContentTuning`
  (1962930); layouts from WoWDBDefs (THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES "Format definitions").
- Rows in encrypted sections of a table cannot be read; `manifest.json` counts them, and they stay
  unknown.
- No DB2 or other client file is committed or deployed; only these derived JSON files are.
