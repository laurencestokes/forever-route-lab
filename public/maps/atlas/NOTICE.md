# Map atlas tiles: notice

Generated by `tools/maps/atlas.ts`. Do not edit these files by hand; regenerate them with `pnpm tsx
tools/maps/atlas.ts` (it needs the pinned client at `WOW_INSTALL`) and check them with `pnpm
maps:validate`.

## What these files are

792 WebP tiles of one continuous map of Kalimdor and the Eastern Kingdoms, with Zephras Isle in a
card between them, composed from 50 of the World of Warcraft world map's painted images (zones,
cities and the Zephras Isle map) of the World of Warcraft: Forever client (`wow_classic_beta`
1.60.1.70009); 2 continent images were read for their land colouring and are not drawn. `index.json`
tells the map which tiles exist; `manifest.json` lists every file with its SHA-256, every source
image with its pixel hash, every parameter, the censuses and the alterations below.

## How the tiles alter the art (D-042 O11)

The owner decided these alterations are covered by composing the paintings seamlessly
(docs/DECISIONS.md D-041 H, D-042 O11). The tiles:

1. Each painting is masked to its terrain polygon, its painted ground (the explored-overlay union)
   and a coastal band, and neighbouring paintings' colour is cross-faded over ±200 yd.
2. Painted labels are hidden by a mirror fill from the same painting: the capital banners at levels
   −1 and 0, where the city plan they name is drawn, and duplicate names at every level (listed
   under "hiddenLabels").
3. This project's own relief-shaded tint fills land that no painting shows.
4. This project's sea colours fill the water outside the paintings' coastal bands.
5. The art is resampled (bilinear, from mip levels; coarser levels are 2×2 box reductions) and
   re-encoded as WebP at quality 80.

  covers part of it at levels −1 and 0, and the plan carries the name

The masks, the tint's relief shading and the sea's distances come from this project's terrain
byproducts (`public/maps/terrain/`, docs/DECISIONS.md D-032), which are derived from the client's
terrain data, not from the painted art.

## Why they are here (D-033)

The project owner decided to publish the painted map art with a notice (docs/DECISIONS.md D-033) and
to compose it into one map (D-041 H, D-042). The owner's rationale is recorded as stated, not
verified: many third-party sites already show Blizzard's map art, and the owner believes Blizzard's
policies allow this for fan sites. The decisions draw no legal conclusion, and the owner accepts the
risk. The governing source D-033 records is Blizzard's Legal FAQ, section "Copyright/Trademark
Policy for the Internet"
(https://www.blizzard.com/en-us/legal/c1ae32ac-7ff9-4ac3-a03b-fc04b8697010/blizzard-legal-faq).

The project follows four rules adopted from that page's conditions:

1. The site stays non-commercial: no ads, paid features or sales.
2. Blizzard copyright and trademark notices accompany the art (this file, the About dialog and the
   README).
3. The art is removed promptly if Blizzard asks. Requests:
   https://github.com/laurencestokes/forever-route-lab/issues.
4. The project never distributes hacks, cheats or similar content.

## How they were made

- Read-only from the local client's `Data/` folder through this project's CASC reader
  (`tools/casc`), pinned to `wow_classic_beta` 1.60.1.70009, build key
  `05215079e3905ef5922ae0b03ffefb73`. Nothing is fetched from the network. Each source image is the
  same lossless raster `tools/maps/convert.ts` composes, checked against the pixel hash the art
  manifest records for it.
- Composited at 4 yards per pixel, reduced by 2×2 box filters for the coarser levels and recomposed
  at 2 and 1 yards per pixel where a painting or city plan is that fine, then encoded as lossy WebP
  by sharp 0.35.4 (libwebp 1.6.0) at quality 80.
- No BLP, DB2 or other client file is committed or deployed; only these derived web images are.
