# Notice: derived navigation data

Generated by `tools/terrain/extract.ts` (`pnpm nav:extract`) from the local World of Warcraft:
Forever client. Do not edit these files by hand; regenerate them with the pinned client and check
them with `pnpm nav:validate`.

## What these files are

- `<mapId>/<row0>_<col0>.bin`: a walkable polygon mesh of Eastern Kingdoms (map 0) and Kalimdor (map 1), one file per block of 4 × 4
  map tiles, in this project's own `FRN3` format (voxel integers, no floating point).
- `<mapId>/map.bin`: per map, the connected components of that mesh, the connector links and the
  tags of unverified passages, in this project's own `FRNM` format.
- `connectors.json`: the connectors the owner observed in game (D-031); `manifest.json`: the pin,
  per-file SHA-256, per-block input hashes, the settings and the other build inputs.

This is **derived data, not game files and not map art**. It was computed by this project from
the Forever client's terrain heights and holes, liquids, per-chunk and WMO area ids, and the
collision of placed objects (WMO and M2 models), read with this project's own read-only CASC
reader from the client's `Data` folder, pinned to product `wow_classic_beta`, version
`1.60.1.70009`, build key `05215079e3905ef5922ae0b03ffefb73` (D-028). No ADT, WMO, M2, BLP or DB2 file, and
no painted map art, is included. The polygons carry top-level zone ids (AreaTable ids) derived
from the same data (D-034 item 3). Which small parts of the mesh are kept also depends on the
QuestieDB-derived dataset in `data/`: a part that a dataset spawn stands on is never pruned
(D-030).

## Accuracy

Walkability is mesh-derived: it has not been checked against the game, and no calibration set
exists (D-031). Elevators, portals and doors are not in the client data; they come from the
owner's observations, and until they are recorded a leg between disconnected parts of a map is a
labelled straight-line estimate with a warning. A spawn census lists every dataset spawn outside
the main connected part of its map, each with a reviewed reason (D-030).

## Owner decisions

- D-028: terrain-aware travel from committed, derived navigation data, extracted with our own
  read-only CASC reader and published with provenance notices, the same posture as the committed
  client values of D-018 and D-022.
- D-030: the lighter mesh (at most 7 MB gzip in total) and the mandatory spawn-census gate.
- D-034: portals and elevators as owner-observed connectors, per-polygon zone ids, and warnings on
  unverified passages.
